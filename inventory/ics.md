# ICs

## LM358

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~12
- Notes: Dual op-amp. Single-supply capable, 3V–32V. General purpose. 10 added from AliExpress batch (2026-06-05) on top of the original 2.

## NE555

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~12
- Notes: Timer/oscillator. Astable/monostable configurations. 10 added from a dedicated AliExpress batch (2026-06-05); the original 2 were from an assortment image and remain unverified by marking.

## JRC4558

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: 2
- Notes: Dual op-amp. Audio-grade, commonly used in effects pedals. Similar pinout to TL072/RC4558.

## LM324

- Package: DIP-14
- Qty: ~5
- Notes: Quad op-amp. Single-supply capable, 3V–32V. Same internal amp as the LM358 (two→four). General purpose.

## LM393

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~10
- Notes: Dual comparator. Open-collector outputs (needs pull-up). 2V–36V single supply. The comparator on the rain-detection driver boards in stock is the same part.

## UA741

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~10
- Notes: Single op-amp. The classic general-purpose op-amp — needs dual supply for full swing, dated but useful for learning/Platt experiments. ±15V typical.

## NE5532

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~10
- Notes: Dual low-noise op-amp. Audio-grade, low distortion, ±3V–±20V. Better noise spec than the JRC4558 for audio/precision work.

## LM386

- Package: DIP-8
- Qty: ~10
- Notes: Audio power amplifier. ~325 mW into 8Ω at 5V, gain 20–200 (settable via pins 1/8). For small speaker driver / tone output experiments.

## ULN2003

- Package: DIP-16
- Qty: ~5
- Notes: 7-channel Darlington transistor array, open-collector, ~500 mA/channel, built-in flyback diodes. Standard part for driving the 28BYJ-48 stepper, relays, and small inductive loads from logic.

## ULN2803

- Package: DIP-18
- Qty: ~5
- Notes: 8-channel Darlington array — same as ULN2003 with one extra channel and a more breadboard-friendly wide pinout. ~500 mA/channel, flyback diodes included. Relay/LED-array/stepper driving from logic.
